A bit about me

I'm a Mechanical Engineering grad from George Mason University (aerospace concentration). I've always been drawn to the making side of engineering — not just designing things on a screen, but building them.

At the MIX Fab Lab, I led the CES booth build from concept to construction, developed and taught workshops on specialized equipment, and keep all the machines running. Over time my role has grown to include lab operations — staffing, events, and planning — while staying hands-on with the equipment every day.

Outside of work I document builds on YouTube and take on projects that push me to learn something new.
